# Beginner testing (Phase 4, step 23)

Automated tests can't tell you whether HL is *easy to learn*. People can. Find 3-5 people who have
never programmed. Sit next to them and say nothing. Give them only `README.md`'s cheat sheet.

## Tasks (15 minutes each, in this order)
1. Make the computer say your name.
2. Ask the user's age and say whether they can vote (18 or older).
3. Count from 1 to 10.
4. Store three foods in a list and print each one.
5. Write a function that doubles a number, then use it.
6. Fix this on purpose-broken program (give them `tests/programs/typo.hum`).

## What to write down for every task
- Time to finish (or "gave up").
- Every place they hesitated, re-read, or guessed.
- Every error message they saw, and whether they understood it **without help**.
- Words they tried that HL doesn't know (e.g. `print`, `=`, `return`, `else`, `true`).

## How to use the results
- A word tried by 2+ people is a candidate for a synonym (e.g. accept `else` for `otherwise`).
- An error message someone didn't understand gets rewritten, then re-tested.
- Success target: 4 of 5 people finish tasks 1-4 unaided; 3 of 5 finish task 5.
