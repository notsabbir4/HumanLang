# Code of Conduct

HumanLang is for beginners, so this community has to feel safe for people who are new, nervous, or
asking a "silly" question. That is the whole standard.

**We expect everyone to:**
- Be kind and patient. Every expert was once a beginner.
- Answer the question that was asked, without making the person feel small.
- Assume good intent, and say so when someone is confused rather than lazy.
- Welcome people of every background, age, language, gender, ability and experience level.

**We do not allow:** harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual or violent language, publishing other people's
private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

**What happens:** maintainers may edit or remove messages and may warn, mute or ban people. If something
happened to you or you saw it, contact the maintainers privately (the address is on the project page), and
you will be listened to.

This applies in the repository, issues, discussions, chat rooms and any space where someone speaks for HumanLang.
